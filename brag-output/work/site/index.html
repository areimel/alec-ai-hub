How it works

Find it, fill it in, copy it.

  1. Step 01

    Find

    Browse by category or search by keyword. Prompts can also be filtered by AI tool.

  2. Step 02

    Fill in

    Some prompts are templates with blanks. Type your details in and the prompt updates as you go. Agents and skills have no blanks, but you can edit their files.

  3. Step 03

    Copy

    Press copy. Paste a prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or whichever tool you use, or paste an agent or skill into the file its page names.
