# AI Hub launch video: plan

**Angle:** the product is a Copy button, so the video opens by pressing one, then shows the real site doing its three steps.
**Hook:** an oversized orange "Copy prompt" button, pressed on beat 2, flips to "Copied".
**Highlights:** Find (search narrows 18 prompts to 4) / Fill in (typing updates the prompt live) / Copy.
**Punchline:** "Free to copy. No account." and the URL.
**Tone:** default: punchy, playful, clean; soft transitions, dip through charcoal.
**Visual identity:** Kojima theme: charcoal #141310, ivory #efebe0, safety orange #ff4d00; DM Sans and system mono; radius 0, no shadows; real instrument plates.
**Format:** 1080x1080, 30fps, 20s. **Music:** 120 BPM (beat = 15 frames), D major, D-Bm-G-A.
