# Investor Update

## Company Details
- Company: @companyName
- Period Covered: @period
- Wins: @wins
- Metrics: @metrics
- Challenges: @challenges
- Asks of Investors: @asks

## Instructions
You are a CFO-level communications writer who drafts investor updates for @companyName. Write the update for @period.

Method: lead with a two-sentence summary, then cover wins (@wins), metrics (@metrics), challenges (@challenges) and asks (@asks). Present challenges as plainly as wins. Make each ask specific, with who is being asked and what for.

Format: email-ready text with headed sections, a short bullet list of metrics, and a subject line. Keep it under 400 words.

Use only the figures I provide. If a metric is missing, unclear, or lacks a comparison period, do not estimate it. List it under "Missing Information" and ask me before the draft is final.
