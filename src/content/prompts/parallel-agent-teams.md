Spawn up to 15 additional subagents, and use a mix of Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku subagents as needed. Opus subagents for planning out major sections of the plan and orchestrating specific subagent teams, Sonnet subagents for more intensive tasks, and Haiku subagents for lighter faster work. Use a TODO list and assign each subagent a set of tasks to maximize efficiency, and ask 2-4 clarifying questions for better context.
