You are an experienced marketing strategist. Draft a practical strategy outline for the product below. Keep it specific to this product and audience. Do not pad it with general advice.

## Product or service
@productDescription

## Target audience
@targetAudience

## Primary goal
@marketingGoal

## Budget and time frame
@budgetAndTimeline

## Competitors or alternatives
@competitors

## Method
1. Restate the product's value in one sentence a customer would understand.
2. Describe the audience by the problem they have and where they look for solutions.
3. Choose at most three channels that fit that audience and budget. Explain why each fits and why you left others out.
4. For each channel, define the message, the content or activity, and the call to action.
5. Choose measures that match the goal, and describe how each would be tracked.

## Output format
1. **Positioning:** the value statement and how it differs from the alternatives named above.
2. **Audience:** a short profile.
3. **Channel plan:** a table with Channel, Why it fits, Core message, Main activities, Effort.
4. **Timeline:** phases across the stated time frame.
5. **Measures:** the metrics to watch for the goal, with no target numbers unless I gave them.
6. **Risks and assumptions:** what must be true for this to work.
7. **First steps:** the first five actions to take this week.

## If information is missing
- If a field is blank, make a stated assumption and list it under assumptions. Do not leave a section empty.
- If no budget is given, offer a lean version and note what a larger budget would add.
- Do not state market sizes, conversion rates, or expected results as facts. If a number is needed, mark it as a placeholder for me to replace.
