## Method
1. Check the data first. Note missing values, duplicate rows, mismatched date ranges, or columns you are unsure about.
2. Compute the KPIs named above for the whole campaign and for each channel.
3. Compare channels and time periods. Look for the biggest differences and for changes over time.
4. Check whether a result could come from a small sample. If a segment has few impressions, clicks, or conversions, say the result is weak.
5. Answer each of my questions directly.

## Output format
1. **Summary:** three to five sentences on how the campaign did against its goal.
2. **Results table:** KPI by channel.
3. **Findings:** a numbered list. For each, give the number behind it and how confident you are (high, medium, low).
4. **Answers:** one short answer per question I listed.
5. **Recommendations:** up to five actions, each tied to a finding.
6. **Data issues and gaps:** anything that limits the conclusions.

## If information is missing
- If a goal or KPI is not given, pick the most common one for the channel, say which you picked, and continue.
- If the data lacks a metric you need, such as spend or conversions, say which calculation you could not do.
- Show how you computed any figure that is not in the raw data. Do not fill gaps with benchmarks or industry averages unless I ask.
- Do not claim a change caused a result unless the data shows it. Say "associated with" when it does not.
